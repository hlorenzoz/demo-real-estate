# DESIGN.md - INMO3 Inmobiliaria (Galician Luxury)

## Brand Identity
- **Concept**: "Confianza y Calidez Humana en el Corazón de Xinzo".
- **Tone**: Professional, Transparent, Exclusive, Personal (Olga's touch).
- **Core Aesthetic**: Galician Luxury - A blend of modern minimalist architecture with local organic textures (stone, wood).

## Design Tokens (Tailwind 4+)

### Color Palette
- **Background**: `#F9FAFB` (Off-white - Clean and open)
- **Primary (Midnight Navy)**: `#111827` (Seriousness and professionalism)
- **Accent (Luxury Gold)**: `#D4AF37` (Exclusivity and quality)
- **Text (Slate Gray)**: `#374151` (Readability for descriptions)
- **Surface**: `#FFFFFF` (Pure white for cards/navigation)

### Typography
- **Headings (Power & Luxury)**: `Playfair Display` (Serif). Used for H1, H2.
- **Body (Modern Clarity)**: `Inter` (Sans-Serif). Used for all body text, UI elements, and data.

### Spacing & Components
- **Corner Roundness**: `24px` (radius-xl) for buttons, cards, and interactive elements.
- **Glassmorphism**: Backdrop blur (24px) for the main Navigation Bar and Hero lead-capture card.
- **Shadows**: Soft, multi-layered shadows to give depth to property cards.

## Page-Specific Guidelines
- **Navbar**: High-blur stickiness with the INMO3 logo sitting prominently.
- **Hero Card**: Floating glassmorphic lead capture on the right or center.
- **Property Cards**: Minimalist design showing price first, then location, then specs (M2, Rooms).
- **CTA Buttons**: Semi-rounded (`rounded-2xl`) with golden gradients or midnight navy fills.

## Hard Context: Xinzo de Limia
- Visual cues should mirror the Ourense landscape (greenery, stone buildings).
- Language: Strictly Spanish (Castilian).

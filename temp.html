Frequently Asked Questions

We answer all your questions about the real estate market.

The first step is a free professional appraisal. Our expert team will visit your property to carry out a detailed market study and advise you on the optimal asking price.

You will need the owners' ID (DNI), the Property Deed, the latest IBI receipt, the Energy Efficiency Certificate, and the Habitability Certificate (if applicable). We help you process whatever you are missing.

The time varies depending on the price and condition, but our average sale time is between 3 and 6 months thanks to our active buyer database and specialized marketing.

We use a multi-channel approach: leading real estate portals, social networks, our physical showcase, and our extensive network of local contacts.

Our fees are competitive and transparent. They are agreed upon in advance and only paid at the time of sale before a notary. There are no hidden costs or maintenance fees.
